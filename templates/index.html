Why Lumen exists

Lumen is a fictional demo store, built to show what a warm, unhurried storefront can feel like. Every product here is imaginary — the presets, templates, and ebooks are placeholders for your own work.

The theme is a single self-contained file: no trackers, no external fonts, no image servers. Just paper, ink, and a little terracotta.

  • Instant delivery — downloads land in your inbox seconds after checkout.
  • Honest pricing — no subscriptions, no upsell mazes, no dark patterns.
  • Made to be remixed — swap the palette, the copy, and the catalog; the layout holds.
